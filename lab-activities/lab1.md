
# Laboratory Task 1: Types of Data Analytics

## Overview
This activity explores the four types of data analytics: descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics.

## Objectives
- Understand the four types of data analytics.
- Apply each type to the given scenario.
- Explain how analytics supports decision-making.

## Activity
**1. Descriptive Analytics**
- How many doses of the AstroJR COVID-19 vaccine have been administered in each country this month?
- How many vaccine doses are currently available in each region?

**2. Diagnostic Analytics**
- Why did the demand for AstroJR's COVID-19 vaccine increase in certain countries?
- What factors caused delays in the distribution of vaccines to some regions?

**3. Predictive Analytics**
- How many vaccine doses will be needed in each country next month?
- How many COVID-19 cases are expected to occur in the coming weeks?

**4. Prescriptive Analytics**
- If vaccine demand is expected to increase, how should AstroJR allocate its available vaccine doses among different countries?
- If some regions are experiencing vaccine shortages, how should AstroJR adjust its production and distribution to meet the expected demand?

## Analysis
The four types of data analytics help AstroJR make informed decisions about vaccine production and distribution.

- **Descriptive Analytics:** Helps AstroJR understand past and current vaccine distribution by identifying the number of doses administered in each country and the available vaccine supply in each region.
- **Diagnostic Analytics:** Helps determine why vaccine demand increased in certain countries and identify the factors that caused distribution delays in some regions.
- **Predictive Analytics:** Helps forecast the number of vaccine doses needed in each country and estimate future COVID-19 cases, allowing AstroJR to prepare for possible increases in demand.
- **Prescriptive Analytics:** Helps AstroJR decide how to allocate available vaccine doses among countries and adjust production and distribution to address shortages and meet expected demand.

## Conclusion
This activity helped me understand the four types of data analytics and how they support decision-making in a real-world business scenario. Descriptive and diagnostic analytics help identify what happened and why it happened, while predictive and prescriptive analytics help anticipate future needs and recommend appropriate actions. Through this activity, I learned how AstroJR can use data analytics to improve vaccine production, allocation, and distribution.
